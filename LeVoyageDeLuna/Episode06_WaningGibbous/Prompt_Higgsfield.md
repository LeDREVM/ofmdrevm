A waning gibbous moon over an African savanna at night, deep indigo sky #1C1A3A.
A small child made of silver moonlight #E8E8F0, glowing slightly softer than

scattered stars on the ground. Sacred, generous, tender, minimalist. Ultra-realistic
cinematic lighting. 4K, 24fps, filmic, slow pacing.
